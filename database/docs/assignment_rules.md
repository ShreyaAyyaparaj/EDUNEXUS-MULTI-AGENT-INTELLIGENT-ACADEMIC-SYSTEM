# EduNexus Assignment & Evaluation Submission Rules

## Submission Rules
- All course assignments must be submitted electronically via the EduNexus portal before 11:59 PM on the specified due date.
- **Late Submission Penalty**: 
  - Up to 24 hours late: 20% deduction from total marks.
  - 24 to 48 hours late: 50% deduction.
  - After 48 hours: Submission rejected (0 marks assigned).

## Plagiarism Policy
- All code and report submissions are scanned through automated similarity checkers.
- Similarity index above 15% will trigger a warning and 50% penalty.
- Similarity index above 30% results in immediate 0 marks and escalation to the Ethics Committee.
