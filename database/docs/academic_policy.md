# EduNexus Academic Regulations & Policy

## Attendance Policy
- **Minimum Requirement**: 75% aggregate attendance per subject is strictly mandatory to be eligible for End-Semester Examinations.
- **Condonation (Medical Exemption)**: Attendance between 65% and 74% may be condoned by the Dean of Academic Affairs upon submission of verified medical certificates within 7 days of illness.
- **Detention**: Students with attendance below 65% in any subject are detained for that subject and must re-register when offered in subsequent semesters.

## Grading System & CGPA Calculation
- Grade S (10 points): 90% and above
- Grade A (9 points): 80% to 89%
- Grade B (8 points): 70% to 79%
- Grade C (7 points): 60% to 69%
- Grade D (6 points): 50% to 59%
- Grade F (0 points): Below 50% (Fail)

Formula for CGPA = Sum(Credit * Grade Point) / Sum(Total Credits)
