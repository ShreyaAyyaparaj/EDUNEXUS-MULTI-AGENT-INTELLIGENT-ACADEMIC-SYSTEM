# EduNexus Placement & Career Guidelines

## Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum CGPA of 6.5 across all semesters with no active backlogs.
- Attendance percentage must be 75% or higher in all enrolled courses.
- Students must have completed at least 2 verified SkillFolio entries (certifications or projects).

## Campus Placement Process
1. **Pre-Placement Talk (PPT)**: Attendance is mandatory for registered companies.
2. **Online Assessment (OA)**: DSA, Aptitude, CS Fundamentals (DBMS, OS, Networks).
3. **Technical Interviews**: 2 to 3 rounds focusing on data structures, system design, and resume projects.
4. **HR Round**: Behavioral assessment, culture fit, compensation discussion.

## Top Hiring Companies & Packages
- **Tier 1 Tech**: Google (32.5 LPA), Microsoft (28 LPA), Amazon (24 LPA)
- **Product Companies**: Atlassian (26 LPA), Flipkart (20 LPA), Adobe (22 LPA)
- **High-Growth Tech**: Swiggy, Zomato, Razorpay (14-18 LPA)
- **Mass / IT Services**: TCS Digital (7.5 LPA), Infosys Power Programmer (9.0 LPA)
